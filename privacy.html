Privacy notice

Last updated 8 November 2026

This notice explains what we do with the information you give us through this website. We collect as little as we can, keep it safe, and delete it when we no longer need it.

Who we are

The Might Not Workshop (TMNW) is a community interest company in formation, setting up free drama and media sessions for young people in Teesside. We are responsible for the information described here.

Our data protection lead is Mark Johnson. You can contact him at mark@themightnotworkshop.co.uk.

What we collect and why

  • Your email address, if you sign up for updates. We use it only to send you news about TMNW, such as when sessions start and how to get involved. We rely on your consent to do this.
  • Anything you send us by email. We use it to reply to you. We rely on our legitimate interest in answering messages people send us.

If you are under 16, please ask a parent or carer to sign up for you.

When young people start coming to sessions, we will give families a separate privacy notice explaining the information we collect at registration.

Who sees it

Only the people at TMNW who need it. We use Mailchimp to store our mailing list and send emails. Mailchimp acts on our instructions under a data processing agreement.

Mailchimp stores data in the United States. It is certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which UK law recognises as giving your information adequate protection. Mailchimp also tracks whether emails are opened and links are clicked, so we can see whether our updates are useful.

We never sell your information. We never pass it to funders or anyone else to market to you.

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser loads them, Google receives your IP address. This site does not set any cookies of its own.

How long we keep it

  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe. Then we delete your email address.
  • Emails you send us: as long as we need them to deal with your message.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you
  • correct it if it is wrong
  • delete it
  • stop using it, or limit how we use it

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clicking “unsubscribe” in any email from us, or by emailing mark@themightnotworkshop.co.uk. We will respond to requests within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, email mark@themightnotworkshop.co.uk. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it without undue delay, keep you updated, and tell you what we find.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice if anything changes, and change the date at the top of the page.
